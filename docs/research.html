Research

My research uses conversation analysis to examine Korean interaction in everyday and technology-mediated settings. I work in two connected areas: how people handle misunderstandings with voice-based AI assistants, and how AI chatbots can support Korean language learning.

Dissertation

When AI Misunderstands: Repair and Trouble Management in Human-AI Cooking (expected May 2027)

The dissertation studies how Korean-speaking users recognize and handle trouble while cooking with a voice-based AI assistant. Using conversation analysis of video recordings, it examines how users coordinate talk with gaze, body movement, and hands-on work.
