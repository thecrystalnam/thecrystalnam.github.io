Research

My research uses conversation analysis to examine Korean interaction in everyday and technology-mediated settings. My dissertation, When AI Misunderstands: Repair and Trouble Management in Human-AI Cooking (expected May 2027), studies how users manage trouble with a voice-based AI assistant while doing something else with their hands.
