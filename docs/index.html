About me

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Korean Linguistics at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. My dissertation uses conversation analysis to study how Korean-speaking users recognize and handle trouble while cooking with a voice-based AI assistant. I am also an ACTFL-certified OPI tester in Korean. From 2022 to 2025 I taught ten Korean courses, using AI chatbots I built myself: Sayromi for individualized speaking practice and Toguly as a debate partner for advanced learners. My interests include interactional linguistics, pragmatics, multimodality, user-centered design for conversational AI, and AI tools for language learning.
