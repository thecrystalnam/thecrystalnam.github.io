Sayromi

Sayromi is a Dialogflow-based conversational chatbot I designed for Korean language learners. I integrated it into elementary and intermediate Korean courses at UH Mānoa (2022-2025) to give students individualized speaking practice, evaluated its classroom use, and published the findings (EALL Working Papers, 2025).
